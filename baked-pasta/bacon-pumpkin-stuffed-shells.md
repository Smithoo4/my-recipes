# Bacon Pumpkin Stuffed Shells

Pasta shells stuffed with a creamy pumpkin, bacon, and gouda filling, topped with more cheese and bacon, then baked until bubbly.

Rating: ★★★ | Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 45 mins | Difficulty: Easy

*pasta, pumpkin, bacon, main dish, easy*

**6 servings, 20 shells**

---

- *12 oz* bacon, chopped
- *1* small sweet onion, chopped
- *2 cloves* garlic, minced
- *2 tbsp* bacon grease (or butter)
- *1 tsp* paprika
- *1 lb* gouda cheese, shredded, divided
- *30 oz* pumpkin puree (2 × 15 oz cans)
- *1 lb* large pasta shells
- salt
- pepper
- chives, chopped, for garnish

---

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F.
2. Add chopped bacon to a skillet over medium-low heat. Cook until the fat renders out and the bacon is crispy. Remove the bacon and drain off the grease, leaving about 2 tbsp in the skillet.
3. Add onion, garlic, and paprika to the skillet. Cook until the onions are soft and translucent.
4. Stir in the pumpkin puree, half of the bacon, and half of the gouda. Combine well, then remove from heat. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Cook the shells in salted water according to the package until al dente. Don't overcook them, or they will be harder to stuff.
6. Drain the pasta and rinse the shells with cold water. Spoon a big spoonful of filling into each shell and place it in a baking dish. Continue until the filling is used up (about 20 shells).
7. Cover the shells with the remaining cheese and bacon. Bake at 350 °F until the cheese is melted and the shells are hot, about 20–25 minutes.
8. Garnish with chopped chives and serve warm.

## Notes

- Add some onions and spice.
