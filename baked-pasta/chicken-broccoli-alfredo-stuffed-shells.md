# Chicken and Broccoli Alfredo Stuffed Shells

Jumbo pasta shells stuffed with chicken and broccoli in a homemade alfredo sauce, topped with mozzarella and baked until bubbly.

Rating: ★★★★ | Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 45 mins | Difficulty: Easy

*pasta, chicken, broccoli, alfredo, main dish, easy*

**6 servings, 24 shells**

---

## Filling

- *12 oz* jumbo pasta shells (about 24)
- *2 1/2 cups* cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie works well)
- *1 1/2 cups* fresh broccoli, steamed and chopped

## Alfredo Sauce

- *3 tbsp* unsalted butter
- *3 cloves* garlic, minced
- *1 cup* heavy whipping cream
- *1 1/4 cups* whole milk
- *2/3 cup* Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
- *3/4 cup* mozzarella cheese, shredded, divided
- *2* large egg yolks, beaten
- salt
- freshly ground black pepper

---

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions, just until al dente. Drain and lay the shells out on a large dish to cool so they don't stick together.
2. **Make the alfredo sauce:** Combine butter, garlic, heavy cream, and milk in a saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a simmer.
3. Beat the egg yolks in a small bowl. Add a spoonful of the hot milk mixture to the eggs, stirring to temper them. Repeat with several more spoonfuls.
4. Slowly add the egg mixture to the saucepan and stir to combine. Remove from heat.
5. Stir in the Parmesan and 1/4 cup of the mozzarella. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. In a large bowl, toss the chicken and broccoli with 1/2 cup of the alfredo sauce.
7. Spread a spoonful of alfredo sauce on the bottom of a 9×13" pan. Fill each shell with a large spoonful of the chicken and broccoli mixture and place it in the pan.
8. Pour the remaining alfredo sauce evenly over the shells. Sprinkle with the remaining 1/2 cup mozzarella.
9. Bake at 350 °F for about 25 minutes, until warm and bubbly.
